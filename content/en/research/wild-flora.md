- A **DNA bank** of about 500 endemic, rare, endangered and wild plant species of Kazakhstan.
- **DNA barcoding** with ITS and matK markers, for example in *Allium*, *Artemisia* and *Oxytropis*.
- The **[kazflora.kz](https://kazflora.kz)** biodiversity database with molecular and botanical records.
- A catalogue of endemic, rare and economically valuable plants of East Kazakhstan (2017).

We work together with 15 national parks and reserves and 5 botanical gardens of Kazakhstan.
