- title: Genetics of agronomic traits in soybean
  image: images/research/soy-gwas-qtl.jpg
  caption: QTL from multi-season GWAS and candidate genes within them, grouped by biological process (Plants, 2026).
  text: 'Soybean cultivars need to match day length and season length. We evaluated a collection of accessions of diverse origin over several seasons in southeastern Kazakhstan for six traits: flowering and maturity time, plant height and yield components.


    Multi-season genome-wide association studies (GWAS) revealed stable QTL. Candidate genes within them are involved in hormone regulation, plant development, photosynthesis and stress adaptation.'
- title: Pre-harvest sprouting in wheat
  image: images/research/wheat-phs.jpg
  caption: 'Scoring pre-harvest sprouting: spikes and grain in Petri dishes (Agriculture, 2026).'
  text: 'When it rains during ripening, grain can sprout right in the spike, lowering flour quality and seed value.


    We evaluated pre-harvest sprouting resistance and agronomic traits in spring bread wheat of diverse origin across contrasting regions of Kazakhstan and used multi-environment GWAS to find genomic regions associated with resistance.'
- title: Stem rust resistance in barley
  image: images/gallery/11-field-2.jpg
  caption: Field phenotyping.
  text: 'Stem rust (*Puccinia graminis* f. sp. *tritici*) is one of the most dangerous diseases of cereals. We scored adult plant resistance in 273 two-row spring barley accessions in two regions of Kazakhstan and ran GWAS with five statistical models.


    We found 204 marker–trait associations and 19 QTL stable across models. Some coincide with the known genes *Rpg1* and *Rpg6*, and a novel major-effect QTL on chromosome 5H is a candidate for marker-assisted selection.'
- title: From markers to cultivars
  image: images/gallery/10-field-1.jpg
  caption: Trial field.
  text: 'We turn the associations we find into KASP markers, which let breeders select the right plants at early stages, before field trials. The lab has co-developed 10 cultivars of wheat, barley and soybean.


    To analyse the population structure of collections and prepare data for GWAS, we develop our own web service, [CropPopStructure](/en/software/).'

We genotype collections on SNP arrays, map genomic regions linked to traits and develop markers for selection. The lab has co-developed 10 cultivars of wheat, barley and soybean.
