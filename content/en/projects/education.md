- **PlantGen 2017.** The lab organised the international conference PlantGen 2017 (Almaty, 29 May – 2 June 2017). Fifteen papers were published in a special issue of *BMC Plant Biology*.
- **Training school "Methodological foundations of plant molecular genetics"** (2016): a 10-day hands-on course on DNA extraction, PCR, electrophoresis, sequencing and data analysis.
- **Training.** More than 30 young scientists trained since 1996; staff supervise PhD, Master's and Bachelor's theses and teach at al-Farabi Kazakh National University.

## Partners

**In Kazakhstan:** KazNIIZiR, Karabalyk and North Kazakhstan agricultural stations, the A.I. Barayev Grain Farming Centre, 15 national parks and reserves, 5 botanical gardens, al-Farabi KazNU, East Kazakhstan and Karaganda universities, the National Center for Biotechnology.

**Abroad:** 19 partner organisations of ADAPTAWHEAT, and partners in China, Mongolia and Israel.
