---
title: "CropPopStructure"
description: "A web app for plant population structure analysis from SNP data."
---

CropPopStructure runs a population structure pipeline from your browser. All computation happens on the lab server, so you don't need a powerful computer.

## How to use it

1. Sign in with your username and password.
2. Upload genotype data as VCF (`.vcf.gz`) or PLINK (`.bed/.bim/.fam`).
3. Choose an analysis: PCA, ADMIXTURE or kinship.
4. When the run finishes, download the tables and plots.

## Access

Accounts are issued on request. Write to us with who you are and what data you plan to analyse.
