Congratulations to Daniyar Yerbolatov! His poster "Rapid speciation and gene flow in *Allium* (Amaryllidaceae)" won the best poster award in the Plant Genetics and Breeding section at **KOGIS 2026**, the first national conference of the Kazakhstan Society of Geneticists and Breeders, held on 13–15 May 2026 in Semey.

Daniyar reconstructed the phylogeny of Kazakhstan's *Allium* species from hundreds of nuclear genes, assessed the roles of incomplete lineage sorting and interspecific hybridization in their evolution, and dated the key stages of the genus's rapid diversification in Central Asia.

Other young researchers from the lab also presented at the poster session. Aruzhan Alikhanova presented her study on the population genetics and species distribution modelling of the rare endemic *Iris kuschakewiczii* B.Fedtsch., and a poster on the genetic diversity of maize (*Zea mays* L.) accessions revealed by SSR markers was also presented.
