Congratulations to our colleagues! Leading researchers **Shyryn Almerekova** and **Alibek Zatybekov** have been awarded the academic title of **Associate Professor (Docent)** in the scientific field 10500 – Biological Sciences.

The title is awarded by the Committee for Quality Assurance in Science and Higher Education of the Ministry of Science and Higher Education of the Republic of Kazakhstan. Alibek Zatybekov received it by Order No. 166 of 6 March 2026.

We wish them new discoveries, strong publications and talented students!
