We study the genetic diversity of Kazakhstan's major crops and look for genes behind yield, grain quality and disease resistance.

- Genetic diversity of wheat, barley, rice, oat, soybean, chickpea and pea with microsatellite markers.
- Genotyping of wheat, barley, rice and soybean collections with Illumina SNP arrays.
- Flowering-time genes (vernalization and photoperiod) in wheat, barley and soybean.
- Resistance to net blotch and stem rust in barley, and to leaf and stem rust in wheat.
- Markers and QTL for grain quality and disease resistance in wheat.
- GWAS of soybean resistance to fungal diseases, and soybean adaptation across three regions of Kazakhstan.

The lab co-developed wheat (Premiera, Zarina), barley (Tlek, Krasnovodopad-100, Shakhristan, Bozman, Kazygurt, Shashbaulym) and soybean (Zara, Danelia) cultivars.
